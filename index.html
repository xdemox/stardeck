How to Play

Goal: Get as close to 20 as you can without going over. Win 3 sets to win the game.

On your turn, you draw from the main deck automatically. Use up to 4 visible side-deck cards to adjust your total.

  • Green: main deck card value
  • Blue: add value
  • Red: subtract value
  • Gold: special (plus-minus, double, flip, force push/pull, tiebreaker)
